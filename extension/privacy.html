Tweet Cleaner privacy

Version 3.2.0 · Updated October 6, 2026

Tweet Cleaner helps delete posts, replies and reposts from your own X account, or remove likes and bookmarks while keeping the posts. It is independent and not affiliated with X or Google. There is no Tweet Cleaner account, advertising, analytics or developer server receiving your data.

Data used by the tool

  • Account identity: the username is read from the Profile link to confirm the signed-in account and separate progress.
  • Page content: post links and repost, like or bookmark indicators are read from the page. The optional adult content filter reads explicit X media warnings. Likes, bookmarks and adult warning indicators may reveal personal interests. Their IDs, operation types and local classification flags are kept only in your browser for cleanup. Media is never opened or analyzed, and no content is sent to AI. Post text and warning text are not saved in progress.
  • Optional archive: selected files are parsed as JSON in memory to extract IDs. Full files and their text are not sent to the developer or stored by the extension.
  • Authentication: during cleanup, the CSRF protection token available in your X session is read and sent only to X, together with the browser's signed-in session. Tokens, cookies and passwords are not written to progress or reports. The extension does not ask for your password.
  • Cleanup activity: IDs, operation types and completed actions are saved in the site's local storage, separated by account and mode (posts, likes or bookmarks), so work can resume. Run counters and failures remain in memory until the page closes, unless you export a report.

Language

The English/Portuguese preference is saved in local Chrome extension storage. Console and userscript versions use X site storage instead. The storage permission saves only the language preference and shares it between the popup and panel. There is no online translation service or Chrome sync. Uninstalling the extension removes this preference, but not progress saved on X.

Storage and transmission

Progress and pending lists use X's localStorage. Other code running on that origin can access this storage. Removal requests are sent directly to https://x.com; X remains subject to its own privacy policy.

An exported report contains the username, IDs, counters, filter and failures; filtered pending items also contain an adult warning flag. It is downloaded to your computer only when you click Export report. The tool does not send it to third parties.

Permissions

activeTab gives temporary access to the tab where you invoke the extension. scripting opens its panel with bundled code. storage remembers the language preference. The extension checks the signed-in account and whether the tab is your profile, your Likes page or the main Bookmarks page on X. It does not request permanent access to all websites.

Your controls

The panel opens in preview and changes X only after explicit confirmation for the selected mode. You can pause or stop; an in-flight request may finish. Removing a like or bookmark preserves the post; you can mark it again through X. To delete local progress, clear X site data in your browser settings. This may also sign you out and remove other site data. Uninstalling the extension does not clear X storage or undo actions.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the publisher's support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing. For a local installation, contact the person who supplied the package.
